Privacy Policy / Datenschutzerklärung

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit anonymousempire.art, on what legal basis, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

1. Controller

Humberto dos Anjos Gesser Neves

2. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you access the site, GitHub's servers automatically collect information that your browser transmits to the server. This includes:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • IP address (stored as server log entry)
  • Time of request

This data is processed for the purpose of delivering the site and ensuring its security and stability (legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub's privacy practices: GitHub Privacy Statement. A data processing agreement is in place via GitHub's standard contractual clauses for transfers to the USA.

3. Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising identifiers.

4. Local storage

The site uses the browser's localStorage only to remember your selected library view (grid, timeline, map, colour). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

5. Third-party resources

The following third-party resources are loaded when you view pages on this site:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — used to load the IBM Plex typeface. Your IP address is transmitted to Google servers to serve the font files. Legal basis: legitimate interest in consistent typography (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Privacy: Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr / unpkg CDNs — used to load the Leaflet map library and a small animation library. Your IP address is transmitted to the CDN operator for the duration of the request. Legal basis: legitimate interest in reliable asset delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • OpenStreetMap tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org) — loaded when you view the Library's Map view. Your IP and the requested tile coordinates are transmitted to OSM servers. Legal basis: legitimate interest in displaying a map (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Provider: OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom. Privacy: OSMF Privacy Policy.
  • Discogs — referenced as the source of release metadata. Links to discogs.com open only when you click them.

We do not embed videos, social-media plugins, or advertising trackers.

6. Contact by email

If you contact us via the email address listed in the Imprint, your message and the email address you sent it from are stored to process the request. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Messages are retained only for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and are not disclosed to third parties.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object (Art. 21)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3))
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77) — in Berlin: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, datenschutz-berlin.de

8. Data security

The site is served exclusively over TLS (HTTPS) to protect data in transit. GitHub Pages automatically provisions and renews Let's Encrypt certificates.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the site or to legal requirements. The current version always applies; the last update date is shown below.
