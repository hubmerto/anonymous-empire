Techno is the only major music genre that systematically removed the human face from its visual identity. What replaced it was not absence but system: grid structures, monochrome palettes, debossed typography, coded catalog numbers, and an ideological commitment to letting the design speak without the designer being seen.

Anonymous Empire catalogues this visual history. The archive spans approximately eight hundred releases across ninety labels and twenty geographic scenes, from Metroplex's first pressing in 1985 to the present. Each entry preserves the artwork, metadata, and label context of a release, organized by the institutional and geographic networks that determined how the music looked.
